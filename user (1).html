FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do payments work?

You pay securely via UPI. Funds are held safely until you approve the freelancer's delivery, then earnings are released to their wallet.

Is there a platform fee?

Freelancers keep 90% of each order. The platform charges a small 10% commission to keep the marketplace running.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If a freelancer doesn't deliver or the work doesn't match the brief, you can open a dispute and our team will resolve it fairly.

How do I withdraw my earnings?

Once earnings are available in your wallet, request a withdrawal to your UPI ID or bank account. Approvals usually take under 24 hours.
